About Umusingi

Umusingi connects people who need building materials with local sellers in any country. Approved sellers publish listings, and buyers contact them directly by phone or WhatsApp.

Sample listings are fictional. Verify sellers and prices before paying. This prototype does not process payments or orders.

Online listings are stored in Firebase Firestore and are shared across devices.
